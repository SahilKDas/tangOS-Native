[smile] Hi, I'm Tango!
This is the Chaos Controller. Every AI you connect shows up as a box here - queue it work and watch it match functions live.

[handsup] Connect an AI
Use MCP connection to open the local client configuration. Add an MCP profile with the client’s exact name; your external client setup remains yours.

[tongue] Hand out work
Pick a batch size and hit "Add to queue" on an AI's box - it gets functions ranked by how close they are to code already solved. A blank count in simple mode keeps it fed until you stop it.

[thinking] Use API keys
Configure your own model/provider profile in Add AI and store its key in Settings. Go runs the configured queue in an isolated worktree.

[smile] Hand-pick in the Viewer
Click functions in the Chaos Viewer (or right-drag a box around them) to fill your cart, then "Add chosen functions" hands them to any AI.

[smile] Pick a role
Give each AI a role from its dropdown. Unassigned uses the adaptive role policy as the pool and recent outcomes change.

[thinking] Dig into an AI
Click any box to pop out its stats - matches, hit rate, tokens, and the function sizes it handles best.

[handsup] The three switches
Writes permits isolated worktree tools. Review every commit and each outgoing push. Port-only fixes must never modify src/; protected inputs and credentials must stay local.

[handsup] Two apps, one toggle
Use the slider up top to flip between the Controller and the Chaos Viewer map of the whole game.
